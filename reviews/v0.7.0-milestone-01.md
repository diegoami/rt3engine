## Findings

1. **Blocking — C5's scenario is not the fixture's consist, and no check can tell.** [#71](https://github.com/diegoami/rt3engine/issues/71) — `docs/milestones.md:122-128` (the claim), `crates/rt3ctl/src/company_cmd.rs:211-233` (`catalogue_consist`), `crates/rt3ctl/src/company_cmd.rs:119-127` (the fixture is read for track and stations only), `crates/rt3ctl/tests/sim_company.rs:58-62` (the check asserts numbers, never the consist's identity), `docs/evidence/rail-economy-v1.md:24-26` and `:72-73` (the document states the catalogue consist and lists the car-to-type assignment as a divergence — so the document and the claim disagree). The claim says the company "runs the fixture's Planet 2-2-0 consist"; the run instead uses the installation catalogue's first four `.car` records behind a locomotive merely *named* `PlanetL`, and `grep -rn 'rail::cars\|rail::trains' crates/` shows no code path reads the fixture's cars or trains at all. **Reproduction:** replacing the CLI's four cars with the fixture's real consist (`PlanetL`, `PlanetT`, `FlatA`, `BoxA` by `id_name`) leaves `cargo test -p rt3ctl --test sim_company` **passing** (`1 passed; 0 failed`) and the CLI output and `state_sha256` **byte-identical** (`2d517847f5b1250fd9fc75cc3f8962b7605df9b92868153337894be8056f7e4c`, revenue 5000.0, cash 3,996.3060493275293). The consist's identity never reaches the state hash or the cash flow; only its car *count* does, and the count happens to be 4 either way. I confirmed the fixture's real consist by decoding its four car records, which name `PlanetL`, `PlanetT`, `FlatA`, `BoxA` — the catalogue's display names for those are `Planet 2-2-0`, `Planet`, `Flatbed` and `Boxcar`.
   **Why blocking:** the claim's scenario identity is a stated, checkable part of C5 and it is false, and the claim's own check cannot observe the falsehood — the gate would not catch a regression of it, which is what `PRINCIPLES.md` (*The six gates disciplines*, item 3) is about. **Why not merely cosmetic:** the C5 money figures are real and the test does catch a change to the cost model and to the car count, so the *numbers* are sound; but the milestone gate is "C1–C6 met on the candidate", and C5 as written is not what runs. The fix is small and is one of two things: build the consist from the fixture's own car and train records and assert its identity in the install-backed check, or correct the claim in `docs/milestones.md` — dated, with its reason in the same commit — to say the typed catalogue's `PlanetL` consist, recording that "the fixture's Planet 2-2-0 consist" was what was wrong, as `PRINCIPLES.md` (*Milestones*) requires for a claim found wrong.
