## Notes for the implementer

- `crates/rt3ctl/src/sim_cmd.rs:411` reports the tiles-style `capacity` for none of the sparse sections — no issue here, but note that `run_compare` prints terrain as `{}x{}` from `rows` then `cols` (`sim_cmd.rs:409`), i.e. "513x321" is 513 rows by 321 columns, while `rt3ctl sim inspect` prints `{}x{}` from `cols` then `rows` (`sim_cmd.rs:310`), i.e. "321x513" for the same map. Both are internally consistent and both are asserted against the artifact, but the two commands order the dimensions oppositely, which is worth a glance before someone compares them by eye.
- `crates/engine/src/economy.rs:122` clamps the cargo `factor` to [0,1]. The evidence doc's rule table says "base and factor from `.cty`" without mentioning the clamp. It is a declared behaviour the doc does not list among its declared-only rules. It does not affect any claim (the 41 shipped factors are within range), but the rule table and the "every economy rule without original-constant provenance" list in `docs/evidence/economy-v1.md:70-81` would be more complete with the clamp stated.
